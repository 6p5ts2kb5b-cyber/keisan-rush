## Promotional Text (170)
Solve math, spelling and kanji questions to power up your insect team! The faster you answer, the stronger you hit. Grades 1–9 on adventure islands.

## Description
Manabi Rush is a learning RPG where every attack is a question. Answer right — and fast — and your insect warriors strike!

■ Answer to attack
• Faster answers land critical hits. Build combos for special moves.
• Wrong answers show a short explanation right away.

■ Three subjects
• Math: from Grade 1 addition to Grade 9 algebra, plus word problems and a Math Test Island.
• English: pick the word for a picture, listen and choose (read aloud), spelling, missing letters, first letters, numbers and everyday replies.
• Kanji — Learn Japanese Kanji: choose the meaning of a kanji, find the kanji for a meaning, count strokes and learn stroke order. Trace kanji with your finger.
• "Learning Balance" shows which subject needs practice. Strong bosses can only be beaten by studying all three!

■ Adventure with insects
• Catch 132 kinds of creatures and fill your Bug Book (360° 3D view).
• Feed them in the Bug Cage — each species eats its real-life food.
• Grow friendship to try evolution (color variants, golden and legendary forms) and fusion!

■ Blacksmith
• Make and upgrade weapons and armor from materials your creatures drop.

■ Safe for kids
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no login, no data sent anywhere.
• Saves stay on the device. Up to 5 save files for brothers and sisters.

## Notes for App Review
No account is needed. On the title screen tap "🌐 English" to use English, then "New Adventure", enter any name, pick a stage and tap Go! Answer questions to attack. All data stays on the device; no ads, analytics or network requests. The camera (Japan Trip mode) only makes a local stamp image and is never uploaded.
